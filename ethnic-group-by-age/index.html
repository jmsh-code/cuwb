Source: Office for National Statistics (ONS), Census 2021, custom dataset: ethnic group (20 categories) by age (6 categories), lower-tier local authority. Group totals reconcile with the published TS021 table to within one to four people; the gap is ONS's deliberate small-cell perturbation, used to protect confidentiality.

Counts under 50 are shown in grey italics. At that size the perturbation can move a figure noticeably, so treat those cells as approximate.

"White British" is the Census category "White: English, Welsh, Scottish, Northern Irish or British".
